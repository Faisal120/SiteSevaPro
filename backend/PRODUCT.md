# SiteSeva Pro

> AI Powered Construction Operating System for India

Version: 1.0 (Pre-Production)

---

# Vision

SiteSeva Pro is an end-to-end Construction ERP + AI Design Studio + BIM Platform built for Indian architects, contractors, builders, developers and project owners.

Our goal is to replace multiple disconnected software systems with a single cloud platform capable of:

- Project Management
- Construction ERP
- AI Floor Planning
- Architecture Design
- Structural Design
- MEP Design
- Quantity Surveying
- BOQ & Estimation
- Construction Monitoring
- Client Collaboration
- Vendor Management

---

# Mission

Become India's most complete Construction Operating System capable of designing, managing, estimating and executing complete building projects.
